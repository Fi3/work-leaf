## Reading the closure labels

- **Closed**: the named candidate-first verification obligation has an evidence-backed
  disposition. This includes a tested-but-not-demonstrated factor, an expressly
  unbounded result, named-cohort nonexposure, a shared behavior, or an observed offset.
  It does not close every possible subfactor in that family or prove a population effect.
- **Coupled**: the observed pathway is real, but its effect is not isolated from the
  stated existing package. The row identifies the missing separation; it does not
  authorize an experiment or assign the package remainder to that member.
- **Missing**: a particular required witness is absent or unavailable. The row states
  what would establish it and whether existing evidence or new authority is needed.
  Absence of the witness is not zero saving.
