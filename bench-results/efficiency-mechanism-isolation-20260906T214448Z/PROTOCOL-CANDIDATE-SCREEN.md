## Mechanism and directional evidence

For each condition, first verify complete actual delivery joins, intended exposure and identity
outside declared ranges. Retain zero exposure as such. Inspect exact public actions and response
identities downstream, including failures and compaction. Candidate-specific mediators are:

- C02: changed/unchanged snapshots and exact delivered bodies, subsequent requested/native reads,
  recovery/edits, context retention/recharge, compaction or request failure.
- C16: actual chosen edit representation, accepted/rejected patches, conflict refresh/re-emission,
  validation and completion responses. Preference noncompliance remains an outcome.
- C24: actual author-fix count, exact original-request insertion and native input item, subsequent
  repair/validation/completion, repeated retained input. No fix means no exposure.

Total raw usage is input plus output across actual generated responses, including cached input;
reasoning is a subset of output. Use the existing strict identity-based accounting and retain
unsupported coverage explicitly. Missing usage is never zero, cumulative repeats are not extra
responses, and accounting corrections are not generation savings. Existing frozen reports are
not overwritten. Fully resolved physical response identities can support a separate same-scope
analysis without pretending their old reports were complete.

The screen reports modified-minus-retained-baseline directional differences only after the
mechanism/identity checks, with any defensible missing-usage bounds. It reports all three results,
not just a favorable one, and never calls this single observation per factor confirmation.
No p-value from the old randomized allocation is imported. A conservative positive contrast
with actual exposure and the proposed action/context chain motivates a separately frozen
fixed-count repetition of that modified variant, using the same saved baseline and preserving
failures. If token bounds alone are uninformative, inspect exact local response/item chains and
identify the remaining measurement boundary rather than inventing a midpoint or extra control.

Percentage attribution belongs to Stage C after the broad mechanism dispositions. A marginal
WL ablation effect is not automatically a share of the historical Direct–WL gap; overlapping
retained-context effects and negative offsets cannot be summed into a fabricated 100%.
Other unresolved candidates remain explicit work after this screen. Keep `../../ephemeral-note.md`
current throughout implementation, diagnostics, benchmarks and analysis; completing the screen
is a checkpoint in the user's continuous investigation, not permission to abandon its plan.
