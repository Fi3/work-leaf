source inverses and existing retention/monitoring/accounting semantics; separate

1. All three named workflows reach the original complete terminal workflow gate,

## What even three favorable outcomes would not resolve

- The counterfactual cost of the six historical Direct specimens under this
  package, or a unique fraction of their historical gap caused by the package.
- Historical CLI 0.149.1/0.150.1 versus current 0.153.4, calendar/provider or
  unobserved effective-state differences, absent an independent comparability
  argument or bound. Three current-treatment samples repeat those differences.
- The marginal parser effect on whole-workflow cost. Pure saved-proposal replay
  already establishes operation-level activation, not a priced alternative
  continuation.
- Arbitrary joint effects outside the declared package, different scheduling
  regimes, missing historical usage bounds, or cancellation of offsets not
  covered by the same-target bridge.
- General task-domain performance, a precise expected 50% saving, or a tightly
  estimated failure probability. Three observations are not a precision study.
- Quality equivalence inferred from identical task text. Under the user's
  premise this remains explicitly conditioned, with all actual frozen failures
  retained. No quality repair or extra quality research is selected here.

Accordingly, **conditional yes for a clearly selected replication question;
no as a sufficient stand-alone closure plan for the existing full historical
claim.** Further action should be chosen for the actual unresolved gate rather
than because three slots are available or the pilot is encouraging.
