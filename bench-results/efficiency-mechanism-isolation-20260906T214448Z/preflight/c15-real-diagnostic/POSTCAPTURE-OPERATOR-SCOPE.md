# Selected closed-source qualification method

The finite requirements in `POSTCAPTURE-PLAN.md` use direct source-bound operator
inspection and the explicitly permitted pinned pure frame/native-index helpers.
No new general-purpose collector or token-accounting framework is required for
this one bounded diagnostic. The complete original analyzer/extractor outputs
remain separate, with one invocation each in their declared prerequisite order.

After closure, root records every actual accepted turn, native thread, generated
directive, private operation and normal accepted-work/check/review exchange. The
operator witness retains exact physical locators, full-byte/hash equality checks,
all source pins and unresolved evidence. A failed or zero-turn attempt stays in
scope; no fixed four-turn transcript or subset of successful proposals is assumed.
Private reasoning content is not exported. Source-bound semantic review compares
every held test with actual accepted source without rerunning tests to force RED.

Read-only hash/identity assertions over saved material are evidence checks, not
an additional provider attempt or accounting extraction. Any later reusable
witness program would require its own test-first implementation and review; this
selection does not declare one implemented or qualified.

The actual outer launcher terminal schema is
`work-leaf-single-diagnostic-terminal-v1`, as defined by `bounded_launch.py`.
Its typed factor/observer conditions, IDs, timestamps and supervised exit fields
follow the generic terminal contract described by the plan. The C21 v5-specific
collector remains inapplicable and is not run on a fabricated v5 input.
