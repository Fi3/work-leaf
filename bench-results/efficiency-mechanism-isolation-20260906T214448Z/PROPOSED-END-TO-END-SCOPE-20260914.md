instruction/information/state differences that cause different decisions and actual
work, quantify their combined contribution without double counting, and provide
the corresponding reproducible non-WL procedure. “Fewer responses” or another

no-quality-loss premise and all actual required validation and repair work.

that one shallow observation rules out an interaction, or that bounded experiments
guarantee a scientific answer. The coverage conditions below must actually pass.
An unrun or confounded experiment cannot supply evidence that the original saving
is false. Failure to explain a gap alone does not prove a benchmark error.
