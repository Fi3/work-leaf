# Candidate dispositions at the broad-evidence checkpoint

This is the Stage B disposition ledger for every C01–C38 and X01–X07 in the frozen [candidate map](CANDIDATE-MAP.md), under the [active plan](PLAN-CANDIDATE-INVENTORY.md). It preserves the accepted H endpoint and the user's no-quality-loss working assumption without using quality to exclude outcomes. It is not a contribution allocation, a rerun of accounting, or an admission. No new controls or Direct workflows are authorized; R's collected large-read variants and their costs remain parked.

H means the accepted six normal-WL/six Direct historical workflows. P is the later two-workflow pilot; S the three-workflow narrow-cue screen; W the twelve-workflow work-unit phase; D bounded delivery diagnostics. The admitted three-variant candidate screen is a separate population. This checkpoint inspects none of its live costs or outcomes. Historical source/CLI qualifications remain in [H lifecycle](EVIDENCE-H-LIFECYCLE.md); the map supplies each exact source owner and call chain.

## What is established—and what is not

- **Local input mechanisms:** H actually delivers bundle manifests, selective native excerpts and compact requested repeats; exact archived bytes establish avoided eager full-text delivery. Later retrieval and retained-input charges can offset that difference. Local bytes are not net workflow tokens.
- **Actual behavior differences:** H Direct executes new-test RED before implementation and substantially more native inspection/focused-check actions. WL packages most initial code/tests together and supplies host/reviewer evidence. These are exercised mediation candidates, not individually identified causes of the whole gap.
- **Collected directional evidence:** the archived bounded continued-response policy increases the saved sample's raw usage; the archived connected orchestration-package comparison retains a substantial difference. Their historical, coupled designs do not identify independent shares or population effects.
- **Offsets against WL:** duplicate instructions/review metadata, title generation, pending-diff resubmission and clean-review parser detours add delivered context or work. The W parser detours have exact recorded response charges; removing their observed branch is not a measured whole-workflow counterfactual.
- **Closed exclusions/inconclusive tests:** absence in H excludes only an exercised H pathway, not the general feature. S/W and old repeat/Git screens remain tested-but-not-demonstrated at their actual scopes. No individual factor here receives a demonstrated historical causal-share label.

“Remaining evidence” below is a concrete unresolved question or a justified no-action disposition, not a requirement to run one experiment per row. Existing artifacts and the already-admitted screen come first.
